New feature: cross-check predicted anniversary reductions against the PBS's own
published indicative figures, and surface where they agree or differ. This adds a
VERIFICATION LAYER on top of existing predictions — it does NOT change any
prediction formula or predicted number. The published figures now sit in the
audit rows from the anniversary_indicative feed (73 rows: per-drug "AEMP as at
1 July 2026" and "Proposed AEMP as at 1 April 2027", split across the s99ACHB /
s99ACJA / s99ACKB sheets for 5/10/15-year).

Do this in two phases. PHASE 1 is investigation — report and stop before building
the UI, because whether this works depends on whether the published rows join to
our predictions.

=== PHASE 1 — CAN THE PUBLISHED FIGURES JOIN TO OUR PREDICTIONS? (read-only) ===

1. MATCH KEY
   The indicative rows identify a drug by "F1 Legal Instrument Drug" + "Legal
   Instrument Form" + "Brand Name" + "Responsible Person". Our predictions key
   off PBS items / drugs. Report: what's the reliable join between an indicative
   row and one of our predicted anniversary reductions? Item code? Drug + form +
   brand text match? How clean is it — exact, fuzzy, or unreliable? Give the
   match rate against our current predicted anniversary events.

2. WHAT THE PUBLISHED FIGURE IMPLIES
   Each indicative row gives old AEMP (1 Jul 2026) and proposed AEMP (1 Apr
   2027). Compute the implied reduction % per row. Report how that compares to
   the rate our predictor would apply for the same drug/anniversary
   (5%/5%/26.1%->30%). Are they consistent, or are there drugs where the
   published proposed AEMP implies a different % than our rate?

3. COVERAGE
   Of our predicted anniversary reductions for 1 Apr 2027, how many have a
   matching published indicative row, and how many don't (we predict it, PBS
   hasn't published it — or vice versa)? Both directions matter: a drug PBS
   published that we didn't predict is as interesting as a mismatch.

Report the join method + match rate, the %-agreement analysis, and the coverage
in both directions. Do NOT build UI yet. I want to know if the data actually
lines up before designing how to show it.

=== PHASE 2 — SURFACE THE CROSS-CHECK (after I approve Phase 1) ===

Do not build until approved. Planned shape:
- For each predicted anniversary reduction, a verification state:
  CONFIRMED (published figure matches our prediction within tolerance),
  DIFFERS (published figure implies a materially different price/%),
  UNPUBLISHED (we predict it, no published row found),
  or PUBLISHED-ONLY (PBS published it, we didn't predict it).
- Show the published old/new AEMP alongside our predicted figure where a match
  exists, so the user can see PBS's own number next to ours.
- Surface this on the prediction/upcoming-changes views without altering the
  predicted values themselves — it's an annotation, not a recalculation.
- A small tolerance for rounding (published AEMPs are rounded); report what
  tolerance you'd use so trivial rounding differences don't show as DIFFERS.

Report the Phase 1 findings first.
